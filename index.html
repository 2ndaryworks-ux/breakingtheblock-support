Common questions

I bought the app before version 2.0. Do I have to pay for Pro?
No. Pro unlocks automatically for anyone who bought the app before 2.0. If it doesn't, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Where are my stories saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your saved stories, so export any you want to keep first (Pro).

Voice notes won't record.
Go to Settings → Breaking the Block and turn on Microphone.
