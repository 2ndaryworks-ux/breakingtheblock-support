Breaking the Block – Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Breaking the Block is made by John Grygo (Secondary Works). This policy explains what the app collects and why.

Your writing stays with you

Your stories and voice notes are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name or email.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone only when you choose to record a voice note. Recordings are saved on your device and are only shared if you export them yourself. You can turn off microphone access anytime in Settings → Breaking the Block.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location based on your internet connection, ad interaction data, and diagnostic data. This data is not linked to your identity by us and is not used for cross-app tracking. In some regions, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Breaking the Block Pro

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment information. Pro removes all ads.

Children

Breaking the Block is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
